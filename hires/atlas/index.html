Planalogy Analog Atlas

We compared every 11 km cell of Earth's dry land with the places where Curiosity, Perseverance and future crews work on Mars, then sent ten search agents and an auditor to find analogs nobody has used yet, and to see which ones climate change creates or erases. The whole search runs in this page: pick a mission and a year, or click anywhere on the map.
